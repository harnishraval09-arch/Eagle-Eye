# Questioned Documents Examination Knowledge

## Physical Examination Methods

| Method | What It Reveals |
|--------|----------------|
| UV/Ultraviolet light | Security fibers, watermarks, fluorescent inks, paper brighteners |
| IR/Infrared reflectography | Ink differentiation, obliterated writing, underlying text |
| ESDA (Electrostatic Detection) | Indented writing, pressure traces on paper |
| Stereomicroscopy (10x–40x) | Printing process, pen strokes, erasures, paper surface |
| VSC (Video Spectral Comparator) | Multi-spectral ink comparison, hidden security features |

## Typography Red Flags
- Mixed font families within a single printed field (e.g. Arial header, Times New Roman in name field)
- Inconsistent baseline alignment across lines — indicates cut-and-paste assembly
- Kerning anomalies in name/date fields — spacing differs from rest of document
- Different resolution artifacts — indicates the document was scanned and reprinted
- Toner vs inkjet mismatch — laser toner where inkjet is expected for document type
- Character weight inconsistency — bold/regular variants mixed unnaturally

## Signature Examination Criteria
Natural handwriting characteristics:
- Variable pen pressure (heavier downstrokes, lighter upstrokes)
- Natural speed tremors present in connecting strokes
- Organic pen lifts at natural break points
- Consistent letter proportions relative to the writer's known specimens
- Slant angle consistent throughout the signature

Forgery indicators:
- Uniform pen pressure throughout (tracing/copying)
- Unnatural pen lifts or hesitation marks
- Slow, deliberate strokes (simulated fluency)
- Blunt stroke endings (tracing lift)
- Letter proportions inconsistent with known specimens
- Retouching or patching visible under magnification

## Paper Analysis
- GSM (grams per square metre): Official government documents typically 90–120 GSM
- Paper grain direction: Affects how paper tears and folds
- Watermark verification: Genuine watermarks are integral to paper, visible in transmitted light
- Security threads: Embedded threads in genuine currency and official documents
- UV fluorescence: Genuine paper has specific fluorescence patterns; copy paper glows brightly

## Ink Analysis
- Ballpoint pen ink ages predictably — solvent evaporation over months/years
- Laser toner cannot be "aged" like ballpoint ink — mismatch is a key indicator
- Inkjet patterns show feathering along paper fibers; offset printing shows dot matrix
- Gel ink shows uniform cross-section; ballpoint shows channel impression
- Carbon copy (OBR) ink has distinct spectral signature

## Printing Process Identification
| Process | Characteristics |
|---------|----------------|
| Offset lithography | Uniform ink layer, halftone dot patterns |
| Laser/Xerography | Toner fused to surface, no ink penetration |
| Inkjet | Ink feathering, visible at 10x magnification |
| Thermal printing | Fades under heat/UV, no ink deposit |
| Intaglio | Raised ink, tactile texture (used in currency) |
